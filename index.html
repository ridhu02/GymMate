Daily Nutrition Suggestions 🥗

Simple food ideas for a healthy daily routine

🥛 Milk

Milk provides calcium and protein that support healthy bones and muscles.

Suggestion: Include a glass of milk in your daily routine if it suits you.

🍌 Banana

Banana provides energy, potassium and fibre.

Suggestion: You can have a banana as a snack.

🥚 Eggs

Eggs are a source of protein and other nutrients.

Suggestion: Include eggs in your meals if you eat them.

🍎 Fruits

Fruits provide vitamins, minerals and fibre.

Suggestion: Eat a variety of seasonal fruits.

🥜 Dry Fruits

Nuts provide healthy fats, protein and energy.

Suggestion: Have a small handful as a snack.

💧 Water

Water helps your body stay hydrated.

Suggestion: Drink water regularly throughout the day.
